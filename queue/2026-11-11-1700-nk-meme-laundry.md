North Korea minted its own meme coins to wash the $6.4B Binance heist.

One homemade token moved ~$100M in a single minute.

The chain 🧵
===
1/ Oct 29: hackers drained Binance hot wallets on 10 blockchains. $6.34B per Binance, $6.43B per Elliptic. The largest crypto theft ever.

The FBI named North Korea's TraderTraitor group. Pyongyang denies it.

The way in: a fake job interview, five months earlier.
===
2/ Stablecoins have an off switch. Tether and Circle froze $2.3B+ in about an hour.

Elliptic now counts $2.85B frozen or seized (44%).

About $3.11B still sits in theft wallets, mostly ETH and BTC, "very likely" still North Korea's.
[image: charts/2026-11-10-binance-haul.png]
===
3/ Bitcoin, Solana, XRP and NEAR have no off switch. So that's where the washing happens.

Elliptic counted 20,000+ wallets on 16 chains and 65,000+ transactions by Nov 7. Roughly one new wallet every 25–30 seconds. "On autopilot."
===
4/ The meme coin trick, per Elliptic:

Mint a token for ~$90. Open a pool. Buy it with stolen coins from wallet set A. Sell it from wallet set B.

Now B's money looks like a lucky meme trade, not a hack.
===
5/ Nov 5–7: six tokens on NEAR. Names like FUK, MEILI, SINU. Logos with animals and a fake "Elon 2028" picture (Musk has no link).

Launderers made every sale of all six. ~117,000 NEAR, about $430K. Tiny next to the haul.
===
6/ The big one was on Tron.

TRM Labs: a creator minted 300M tokens to a seller wallet, which sold them the next minute and pulled out nearly $100M in receipt tokens.

After Bybit in 2025, one homemade Tron token moved $24M+. The trick is growing.
===
7/ Why it matters beyond Binance:

TRM says North Korea stole $7.4B in crypto in 2026 alone, 85% of all hack value (39% in 2024).

And Elizabeth Warren is expected to chair Senate Banking from January, with subpoena power.
