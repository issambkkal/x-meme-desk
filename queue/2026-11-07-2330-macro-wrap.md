1. US productivity jumped
2. Berkshire is buying again
3. The FinCEN order against MEXC is freezing deposits at other exchanges
===

Growth without wage inflation. That gives the Fed room to cut, and cheaper money has always been crypto's fuel.
===
2/ Berkshire bought back $9.6B of its own stock in Q3 ($14.4B so far in 2026) after pausing for almost 2 years.

The most patient money in the world is buying, while crypto's Fear & Greed index sat near 28 this week.
===

If you trade meme coins, keep trading funds in a wallet you control. A frozen deposit can't sell.
