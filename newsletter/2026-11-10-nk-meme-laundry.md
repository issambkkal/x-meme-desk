# North Korea is printing meme coins to wash the biggest heist in crypto history

*The $6.4 billion Binance theft is being laundered through tokens the thieves mint, buy and sell to themselves. Here's how it works, and what it says about the meme coin machine.*

On a normal day, a brand-new meme coin with a cartoon logo and a joke ticker is just noise. Between November 5 and 7, six of them on the NEAR blockchain were something else: a laundry. [Elliptic reports](https://www.elliptic.co/insights/north-korea-meme-coins-binance-hack/) that wallets tied to the Binance theft minted the tokens, bought them with stolen coins and sold them back to themselves. Each launch cost about $90.

The meme coin machine, built so anyone can launch a token in a minute, is now a tool of a sanctioned state.

## What happened

On October 29, hackers drained Binance hot wallets across ten blockchains. Binance puts the loss at [about $6.34 billion](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/30/binance-arranges-usd6-34-billion-to-cover-losses-from-record-crypto-hack); [TRM Labs](https://www.trmlabs.com/resources/blog/north-korea-has-stolen-usd-128-billion-in-crypto-usd-74-billion-of-it-in-2026) says $6.36 billion and [Elliptic](https://www.elliptic.co/insights/how-north-korea-is-laundering-the-6-4-billion-stolen-from-binance/) $6.43 billion. It is the largest crypto theft on record.

Binance says the attack started five months earlier with [a fake job interview](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/04/binance-hack-north-korea-fake-job-interview/): an engineer got a "coding test" carrying malware. On October 31 [the FBI named](https://therecord.media/fbi-blames-tradertraitor-for-binance-hack) North Korea's TraderTraitor group. Pyongyang denied it.

Tether and Circle [froze more than $2.3 billion in about an hour](https://247wallst.com/investing/cryptocurrency/2026/10/30/tether-and-circle-froze-2-3-billion-in-hours-after-the-binance-hack-could-they-freeze-yours/). Elliptic now counts $2.85 billion frozen or seized (44%) and about $3.11 billion still in theft-linked addresses, "very likely" still held by North Korea.

The six NEAR tokens (names included FUK, MEILI and SINU, with animal logos and a fake "Elon 2028 President" image, [per Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/382873/binance-hack-elon-musk-animal-meme-coins-launder-stolen-crypto); Musk has no link) moved about 117,000 NEAR, roughly $430,000. The launderers made every sale of all six. About 11.8 million stolen NEAR (~$43.7 million) had not moved.

The bigger number is on Tron. [TRM Labs](https://www.trmlabs.com/resources/blog/binance-hack-update-north-korea-launders-proceeds-through-self-minted-tokens-and-chained-bridges) describes a creator minting 300 million tokens to a seller wallet, which sold them the next minute and withdrew nearly $100 million in receipt tokens. After the 2025 Bybit theft, a homemade Tron token moved at least $24 million.

## Why it works

**Freezes push money to chains nobody can freeze.** Stablecoins have an issuer with a stop button. Bitcoin, XRP, Solana and NEAR don't.

**A self-made token turns stolen coins into "trading profits".** Buy from wallet set A, sell from wallet set B, and B's money looks like a lucky meme trade.

**It's automated.** Elliptic counted 20,000+ wallets on 16 blockchains and 65,000+ transactions by November 7, about [one new wallet every 25–30 seconds](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/03/north-korea-binance-elliptic-chainalysis.html). Elliptic's Tom Robinson called it laundering "on autopilot".

**Bridges carry the load.** [THORChain processed more than $46 million](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/11/01/thorchain-pause-delays-binance-hack-laundering-chainalysis-says/) in the first 72 hours, per Chainalysis. The FBI asked node operators to block listed addresses; THORChain's front end did, node operators made no promise. [RUNE fell about 20%](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/investing/46141).

**The stakes keep rising.** TRM counts $7.4 billion stolen by North Korea in 2026 alone, 85% of all crypto hack value, up from 39% in 2024. ([Chainalysis](https://cryptobriefing.com/chainalysis-north-korea-hacks-89-percent-losses/) says ~89%.) And with Democrats taking the Senate, Elizabeth Warren is [expected to chair Senate Banking](https://cryptobriefing.com/warren-crypto-investigation-trump/) from January, with subpoena power.

## What it means for meme traders

A new coin with rising volume can have zero real buyers. The same week, [Bubblemaps alleged](https://x.com/bubblemaps/status/2119823688673751416) that Solana trader Cupsey pulled about $1.74 million through ~30 linked wallets that sold into copy traders (an allegation; we found no reply from him). Same mechanism: your own wallets on both sides of a trade.

All of this landed in a weak market: spot bitcoin ETFs [lost ~$1.28 billion](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/11/09/bitcoin-price-today-btc-falls-to-74k-as-1-04b-crypto-liquidations-hit-longs-etfs-bleed-1-28b/) in early November, bitcoin hit about $73,500 on Nov 8, and stablecoin supply fell [$4.68 billion last week](https://x.com/lookonchain/status/2119794172752441802).
