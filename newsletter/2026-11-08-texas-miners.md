# Texas just voted against the AI trade. Bitcoin miners already sold their bitcoin to make it.

*Miners sold most of their bitcoin to become AI landlords. Texas's new governor wants to pause the data centers they were building.*


For two years, America's listed bitcoin miners had one answer to a bad business: stop
selling hashpower to the bitcoin network and start renting power to AI companies. To pay
for that switch, many of them sold the bitcoin they had kept for years.

On Tuesday, Texas elected a governor who wants to pause new data centers. Most of that AI
plan runs through Texas. Here is the chain, with the numbers, and why it matters to bitcoin.

## What happened (the numbers)

On Nov 3, Democrat Gina Hinojosa won the Texas governor's race after promising to pause new
data center approvals until the Legislature passes protections for residents, and to make
data centers pay their own power costs ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/04/ai-stocks-texas-governor-data-centers.html),
[Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/04/data-center-texas-pause-gina-hinojosa-governor-policies/)).
She takes office in January. The same night, Democrats won both houses of Congress, and
Sen. Bernie Sanders called for a national data center moratorium ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/05/democrats-data-center-restrictions-midterms.html)).

On Nov 4 the market priced it in. Closing moves, as reported by
[Equibles](https://equibles.com/research/texas-data-center-moratorium-which-stocks-are-exposed-and-how-much)
and [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/trading-ideas/movers/26/11/62882663/hut-8-stock-sinks-after-democratic-midterm-wins):

- Hut 8: −23.2% (it also reported earnings that day)
- Cipher: −20.0%
- Galaxy: −17.0%
- CleanSpark: −16.6%
- IREN: −11.2%, to $21.71 ([FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/iren-stock-near-18-3-before-tonights-q1-results-as-bernstein-cuts-target-to-55/))

Bernstein cut price targets on seven miners and AI hosts the same day: IREN to $55 from
$100, Riot to $18 from $35, Cipher to $15 from $23, MARA to $10 from $17 and CleanSpark to
$11 from $24. It kept its ratings but now values the AI hosting business at roughly half
the earlier multiple, citing "Texas uncertainty on pipeline approvals and higher cost of
capital" ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/04/wall-street-cuts-bitcoin-miner-price-targets-after-democrats-texas-win-threatens-ai-data-center-boom),
[Blockspace](https://blockspace.media/insight/bernstein-cuts-ai-infrastructure-targets/)).
KBW downgraded Cipher and CleanSpark, and Morgan Stanley flagged Texas risk at Galaxy,
Cipher and MARA ([Blockspace](https://blockspace.media/insight/kbw-downgrades-cipher-and-cleanspark/)).

Jefferies puts the at-risk number at 13.3 gigawatts of announced Texas data center projects,
about a quarter of planned US capacity ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/dallas-ft-worth/data-center-development/13-gw-at-immediate-risk-as-texas-data-center-developers-face-new-power-struggle),
[Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91634444/texas-wanted-to-power-americas-ai-boom-its-new-governor-could-change-everything)).
That is an estimate of risk, not a confirmed loss.

## Why it happened (the chain)

**1. Mining one bitcoin costs about what it sells for.** CoinShares put the average
listed miner's cost at roughly $80,000 per bitcoin in Q1; JPMorgan's all-in estimate is
about $78,000 ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/bitcoin-price-prediction-miners-spend-215727079.html),
[Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/jpmorgan-bitcoin-mining-costs-worsened)).
Bitcoin traded near $75,800 on Saturday after touching $74,762 on Friday, its lowest
since Aug 21 ([SecureShift](https://secureshift.io/blog/crypto-market-today-november-7-2026-weekly-recap)).
Hashprice, what one petahash earns per day, was $32.86 on Nov 2, down 8.7% in a week
([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoins-mining-hedge-market-prices-a-winter-pause-after-difficultys-16-rebound/)).

**2. So miners became landlords for AI, and sold bitcoin to pay for it.** Riot's Q3 shows
the trade in one table: mining revenue fell to $116.0M from $160.8M a year earlier because
its average bitcoin price fell to $70,855 from $114,361. It sold 20,264 BTC this year for
about $1.5B, mostly to fund data centers, and its holdings fell from 11,380 to 2,418 BTC
in the third quarter alone ([Riot](https://www.riotplatforms.com/riot-platforms-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results-and-strategic-highlights/),
[TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/business/49038)). Core Scientific sold 816 BTC
in Q3, holds 32, and says it will quit mining by year-end; about 92% of its revenue now
comes from AI customers ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/core-scientific-to-quit-bitcoin-mining-by-year-end-as-ai-business-surges),
[24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/07/core-scientific-is-quitting-bitcoin-mining-as-it-now-makes-about-92-of-its-revenue-renting-to-ai-customers/)).
Across the sector, listed miners sold a record ~32,000 BTC in Q1 alone
([BlockEden](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2026/04/22/public-bitcoin-miners-32k-btc-liquidation-ai-pivot-hashrate-security)).

**3. Texas was the cheapest place to make that switch, and it was already closing.** On Aug
3, Gov. Greg Abbott had ERCOT pause energizing new data centers and crypto mines of 75 MW or
more pending an audit; in September he halted state environmental permits for data centers
([TEI](https://tei-energy.org/articles/2026-08-17-ercot-large-load-pause-verification),
[Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/daraabasiita/2026/10/13/texas-orders-data-center-audit-as-grid-requests-reach-474-gigawatts/)).
The market expected that pause to end. Bernstein's point after the vote: under Hinojosa it
will likely last, and any fix in the Legislature will stall.

**4. Why voters cared.** Power bills. Hinojosa also called for a freeze on residential
rates, and Jefferies lists shared transmission costs and sales-tax breaks (which miners also
use) as further risks ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/dallas-ft-worth/data-center-development/13-gw-at-immediate-risk-as-texas-data-center-developers-face-new-power-struggle),
[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/texas-governor-impact-state-bitcoin-181755705.html)).

## What on-chain and market data show

- **Treasuries are mostly gone.** The big "miner dump" fear is smaller than it looks for the
  leaders: Riot has ~2,418 BTC left and Core Scientific 32. What is left to sell is new
  production, every month.
- **The network is not growing.** Difficulty was 148.36T on Nov 4, and the next adjustment,
  around Nov 13, is estimated at −2.6% to −3.2% ([Pickaxe](https://www.pickaxe.io/resources/news/bitcoin-mining-hashrate-holds-steady-amid-difficulty-shift)).
  A cut means hashrate is flat to falling: fewer machines are being added than switched off or moved to AI.
- **Big money is leaving at the same time.** US spot bitcoin ETFs lost $430.2M on Nov 5 and
  $411.9M on Nov 6 ([Crypto Times](https://cryptotimes.io/2026/11/07/bitcoin-price-today-btc-holds-near-76000-as-spot-etfs-shed-411-9-million), Farside data).

## Who won, who lost

**Won (for now):** Projects that already have permits. Riot signed a 15-year, 910 MW lease
for its whole Corsicana, Texas campus, worth about $28B, with an unnamed "frontier AI lab"
([Riot](https://www.riotplatforms.com/riot-platforms-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results-and-strategic-highlights/)),
and its stock rose on Nov 4 while peers fell. Miners with little Texas exposure, such as
TeraWulf, Applied Digital and MARA, fell less than 7% ([Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/texas-voted-to-pause-new-data-centers-which-bitcoin-miners-are-already-past-the-freeze/)).

**Lost:** Firms whose growth is still waiting on Texas. Hut 8 has 1,000 of its 1,330 MW
under construction at one Texas site, Beacon Point ([Equibles](https://equibles.com/research/texas-data-center-moratorium-which-stocks-are-exposed-and-how-much)).
IREN's 2 GW Sweetwater hub is still conditional on ERCOT's review, and IREN pushed its
$4B revenue run-rate target to Jan 31, 2027 ([IREN](https://iren.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/iren-reports-q1-fy27-results)).
And shareholders who bought these stocks as a cheap way to own both bitcoin and AI found
out they own neither the bitcoin (sold) nor a sure AI pipeline (paused).
