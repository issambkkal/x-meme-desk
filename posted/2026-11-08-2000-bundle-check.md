Free rug check in 30 seconds: open the coin on Bubblemaps.

Look for clusters of wallets linked to each other that hold a big share of supply. Bubblemaps flagged 50% of one launchpad's tokens as bundled at launch.

Linked wallets can all sell at once. You can't sell first.
