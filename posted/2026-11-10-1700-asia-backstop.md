Taiwan put a $15B rescue fund under its stock market and cut short selling 90%.

Crypto took the same AI chip crash with no rescue at all: $1B+ liquidated in one day.

The chain 🧵
===
1/ Nov 5: the US chip index fell 12.25%, its worst day since March 2020. Nvidia −9.26%.

The trigger: after the midterms, markets priced slower AI data-center building.

Asia got the bill the next morning.
[image: charts/2026-11-10-chip-crash.png]
===
2/ Nov 6, Seoul: the Kospi fell 8.55% in minutes and tripped a circuit breaker. Trading stopped for 20 minutes.

Then the national pension fund and local institutions bought, and it closed back above 5,000.
===
3/ Nov 6, Taipei: the Taiex had a record intraday drop of 3,737 points.

Worst week since 2001: −12.19%. Down 20.55% from its Oct 6 peak. Bear market.

Foreigners sold $45B+ of Taiwan stocks this quarter.
===
4/ Taiwan's answer:

• NT$500B (~$15B) state fund, its 10th rescue since 2000
• short-sale cap cut from 30% of volume to 3%
• short margin raised to 130%
• companies told to buy back stock

Monday: Taiex up as much as 5%, TSMC about 7%.
===
5/ And Japan confirmed Monday it spent ¥15.4 trillion (~$97B) over three days this summer to prop up the yen. One day, July 30, was a record ¥7.98T.

Asia's governments have deep pockets and use them.
===
6/ Crypto's version: nobody.

Bitcoin hit ~$73,761 late Sunday, lowest since August. Over $1B liquidated in 24h, ether ($448M) almost double bitcoin ($272M).

Longs were ~90% of the damage, per CoinGlass data.
===
7/ The biggest single victim, per EmberCN: a whale with $250M+ in longs lost about 63,000 ETH ($135M) in one liquidation around 6am.

Loss: roughly $49M–61M depending on who counts.

There is no circuit breaker on a perp.
===
8/ The other buyers stepped back too.

US spot bitcoin ETFs: −$1.28B in net outflows, Nov 2–6.
Fear & Greed: 25, extreme fear.
Fed: ~80% odds of a December hike, not a cut.
[image: charts/2026-11-10-etf-week.png]
===
9/ What to watch:

• today: TSMC's October sales, US CPI reaction
• Nov 17: Nvidia results, first since the CEO probe
• Dec 8–9: Fed meeting

If Asia holds and bitcoin doesn't, crypto has its own problem. Not advice.
