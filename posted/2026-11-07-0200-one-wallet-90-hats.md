Per @bubblemaps: 90 brand-new wallets, all funded from one address, bought 90% of $MYSTERY at launch.

They called it a "textbook scam." The price fell about 75% from its $7.5M peak.

This setup is everywhere in the trenches. How to spot it in 30 seconds 🧵
===
2/ Put the coin on a bubble map before you buy.

Lots of separate holders is normal.

A tight cluster of wallets linked by transfers, sitting on a big slice of supply, is not.
===
3/ Check where those wallets got their first SOL or ETH.

If dozens were funded by the same address right before launch, that's not a community. It's one person wearing 90 hats.
===
4/ Big names aren't exempt.

Bubblemaps says about 30% of PEPE's genesis supply sat with one wallet cluster, which sold around $2M the day after launch.
===
5/ A clean map doesn't make a coin safe. Clean coins still die.

But a messy map is a free "no", and free is the best price in this market.
