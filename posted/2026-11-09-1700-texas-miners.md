Bitcoin miners sold most of their bitcoin to become AI landlords.

Then Texas elected a governor who wants to pause new data centers.

Most of the AI plan runs through Texas. Here's the chain, with numbers 🧵
===
1/ Mining a bitcoin costs about what it sells for.

Average listed miner: ~$80K per coin (CoinShares). JPMorgan's all-in estimate: ~$78K.

Bitcoin hit $74,762 on Friday, its lowest since Aug 21.
===
2/ So miners started renting their power to AI companies, and sold bitcoin to pay for it.

Riot: 11,380 BTC on Jun 30, 2,418 on Sep 30.
Core Scientific: 32 BTC left, quitting mining by year-end.

Public miners sold a record ~32,000 BTC in Q1 alone.
[image: charts/2026-11-09-miner-btc-sold.png]
===
3/ Texas was the cheap place to do it. It was already closing.

Aug 3: grid operator ERCOT paused hooking up new data centers and crypto mines of 75 MW+.
Sept: state permits for data centers halted.

The market expected that to end.
===
4/ Nov 3: Gina Hinojosa won the governor race promising to pause new data center approvals until lawmakers pass protections for residents.

Jefferies: 13.3 GW of announced Texas projects at risk, about a quarter of planned US capacity.
===
5/ Nov 4, the stocks:

Hut 8 -23.2%
Cipher -20.0%
Galaxy -17.0%
CleanSpark -16.6%
IREN -11.2%

Bernstein cut 7 price targets, IREN to $55 from $100. Hut 8 also reported earnings that day.
[image: charts/2026-11-09-texas-miner-drop.png]
===
6/ Who held up: projects already permitted.

Riot signed a 15-year, 910 MW lease for its Texas campus, ~$28B, and rose while peers fell.

Who's stuck: Hut 8 has 1,000 of 1,330 MW being built at one Texas site.
===
7/ What it means for bitcoin:

The big treasuries are mostly sold already. What's left is new coins, sold every month.

The network isn't growing: next difficulty adjustment ~Nov 13 is estimated at -2.6% to -3.2%.

ETFs: -$842M on Nov 5-6.
===
8/ What to watch:

Nov 10: US CPI (a Dec Fed hike makes financing dearer)
~Nov 13: difficulty adjustment
December: ERCOT audit reports
Dec 9: Fed decision
Jan 19: Hinojosa sworn in

Not advice. No coin calls.
