The Bank of England just hiked rates for the first time since 2023.

The reason wasn't wages. It was oil.

The Fed, the ECB and the Bank of Japan have all hiked since September too. Here's how $100 oil ends up draining meme coins 🧵
===
1/ Brent touched ~$100 on Friday after Iran claimed more tankers hit mines in Hormuz.

Nobody has confirmed those attacks. Doesn't matter much: Iran says the strait is closed, and Polymarket gives ~7% odds traffic is normal by year end.
===
2/ Oil is about 54% above a year ago (Trading Economics).

US gasoline: +34.2% in a year (BLS).
Euro area energy: +20.8% (Eurostat).

That's most of the inflation right there.
[image: charts/2026-11-08-energy-inflation.png]
===
3/ So the central banks are hiking into it:

BoE: 4.00%, raised Thursday, 8–1 vote
Fed: 3.75–4.00%, Dec 9 hike odds ~70–80%
ECB: 2.50%, Nagel says a Dec hike is "very likely necessary"
BoJ: 1.25%, 80%+ of economists expect a Dec hike
===
4/ Crypto is at the far end of that chain.

Wednesday, oil fell on Iran-deal hopes: $BTC ~$80K.
Friday, after the tanker claims, a tech rout and hot jobs data: ~$75.5K.

Bitcoin trades on rates, not on "safe haven" headlines.
===
5/ The cash that crypto runs on is leaving:

Bitcoin ETFs: -$502M net for 2026, assets down ~15% since January (SoSoValue)
Stablecoins: $291.6B, -4.6% in 30 days (DefiLlama)
[image: charts/2026-11-08-liquidity-drain.png]
===
6/ Meme coins feel it first.

Pump.fun fee revenue in Nov 1–6: ~$1.26M a day vs ~$2.0M in October. About 37% lower (DefiLlama).

$DOGE sits ~90% below its high. The Trump coin, ~98% (BeInCrypto).
===
7/ The casino still wins, though.

Pump.fun has ~$322M of 2026 revenue, 2nd only to Hyperliquid among crypto projects.

Cash holders win too: they now earn ~4% to sit and wait.
===
8/ What to watch (Doha time):

Tue Nov 10, 16:30: US CPI (monthly number; no yearly figure this time)
Fri Nov 13: US PPI
Dec 9: Fed
Dec 17: ECB + BoE
Dec 18: BoJ

And Hormuz. A real reopening deal is the biggest upside risk. Not advice.
