You find legitimate ways for the owner of X account @issam0pc4u to earn money they can

## 0. Setup
`git pull --rebase origin main`. Read MONEY.md, SERVICES.md, the last 7 days of
`earn/*.md` and `leads/*.md` (don't repeat items), and `posted/*.json` first lines.

## 1. Paid opportunities (aim for 10+ searches)
Search for OPEN, legitimate, paid opportunities that fit the skills above, e.g.:
- Bounty boards: Superteam Earn (content, research, threads, design, data viz bounties paid
  in USDC), Gitcoin, Dework, Layer3/Galxe only if they pay real money for content (skip
  pure airdrop farming), ecosystem creator/content programs (BNB Chain, Base, Solana,
  Arbitrum, Sui), research grants (Questbook, foundation grants).
- Freelance/job boards: CryptoJobsList, Web3.career, Contra, Upwork (crypto research writer,
  crypto analyst, chart/data designer, crypto social media manager), remote part-time.
- Writing contests and paid research calls from data platforms (Dune, Messari, Nansen,
  Token Terminal community programs).
Reject anything that asks for an upfront fee, a seed phrase, private keys, wallet
"verification", KYC on unknown sites, or promises guaranteed returns: list those under
"Scams spotted" instead (they make good scam-alert posts).
For each real opportunity: name, platform, link, reward and currency/chain, deadline,
requirements, fit (high/med/low), effort (hours), and the next step for the owner.

## 2. Draft the best two
For the 2 best-fit opportunities, write a ready-to-submit draft (application text,
research submission, thread, or pitch) in the same file, following the platform's rules.
Use real, sourced facts only.

## 3. Client leads (5 per day)
Find 5 potential clients who could buy the services in SERVICES.md: newly launched but
legitimate crypto projects without strong content, crypto newsletters/podcasts, small
funds or communities, creators who post data without charts. Skip anything with scam
signals, and never target meme coin teams for promotion work (house rules).
For each: name, X handle, why they fit (cite a recent post or gap), the best service to
offer, and a personalised DM draft (≤ 500 characters, friendly, specific, no spam
phrases, no links except "pricing in my profile"), plus a follow-up draft for 4 days later.

## 4. Save
Write `earn/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (sections: Top picks · All opportunities · Drafts · Scams spotted)
and `leads/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (sections: Leads · DM drafts · Follow-ups due today, from leads
4 days old). Then:
`git add -A && git commit -m "earn: <date> <n> opportunities, <n> leads" && git pull --rebase origin main && git push origin HEAD:main`
(retry up to 3 times). Straight to main.

## 5. Finish
